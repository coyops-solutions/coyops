Common questions

Things boards ask us most often.

Honest answers to every question we hear before a community decides to work with us.

Q"Will this replace our existing staff?"

No. What we build helps your staff work more efficiently — not replace them. Automating repetitive tasks means your team spends time on things that actually require human judgment. Most communities find their staff are relieved, not threatened.

Q"Is it difficult to use? Our team is not technical."

We design specifically for non-technical users. If your board members can send a message or book something online, they can use what we build. We also train everyone until they are comfortable — and we document everything so future board members can get up to speed quickly.

Q"Can older residents use the new systems?"

Yes. We design for the least tech-comfortable user in your community — not the most. Systems are simple, visual, and mobile-friendly. We also provide printed guides for residents who prefer paper. Nobody gets left behind.

Q"Will our existing records be lost or moved?"

No records are lost or deleted. We help you organize and migrate what already exists into the new system — at your pace. Everything from the old system is preserved until you are completely comfortable with the new one.

Q"Will this work on mobile phones?"

Yes — everything we build is designed to work on any device: phones, tablets, and computers. Residents can submit requests, check announcements, and book amenities from their mobile phones without downloading any app.

Q"How long does implementation take?"

The assessment takes 2 weeks. A full Community Operations System is typically delivered in 4 weeks. We work at a pace that respects your board's time — and we handle the heavy lifting so you do not have to manage the process yourself.

Q"We need board approval before committing. How does that work?"

Completely understood — we work with HOA boards all the time. After our free call, we prepare a one-page summary of scope, timeline, and investment that you can bring directly to your board. Everything is documented and presentation-ready.

Q"What happens after the project is finished?"

We document everything so thoroughly that your team can manage it independently. Our ongoing Operations Partner plan is available for communities that want continuous support. Either way, you are never left with a system you do not understand.
